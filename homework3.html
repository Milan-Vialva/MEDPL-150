Reflection

I assembled some of my belongings: my notebook, lip gloss, headphones, and pill case. The pill case is the object that’s supposed to be in focus at the center of the photo. I realized that when taking a picture, the shallow depth of field was slightly brighter, and I also had to adjust the camera focus to make it focused on the pill case and get the detailed drawings on it, whereas the deep depth of field was darker, and everything was supposed to be in focus. I also noticed that when changing the ISO, you need to change the f-stop and aperture in tandem to get a balanced, natural-looking photo. I changed the F3.5 to 10, which made the image too dark, so I brought up the ISO from 200 to 400, which made the image more visible. Especially when it comes to the lighting in which the image is taken, you have to play around with the settings and different angles to get the image clear. In Photoshop for the ‘Photoshop Blur’ photo, I used the blur gallery, field blur tool, and clicked on the pill case to focus specifically on that object, and blurred the objects on the outside of it. I also used the easer tool to help clean up some of the image to make sure parts of the pill case weren’t blurred, and the contrast would be visible. I compared the before and after images to make sure the final product seemed natural, so that you can still see what the blurred objects are.
