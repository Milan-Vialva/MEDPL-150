Vector Emotive Eye & Illustrated Environment

Curious Emotive Eye

For the emotive eye, I created a confused expression, which is evident in the curved eyebrow and the pupil and iris being slightly off-centre. I used a real image of an eye to create the pupil, and I used an image of the animated character Vanessa from The Little Mermaid as a reference for the expression I wanted to illustrate. The circle and star shapes were used for the pupil and iris, and the straight and curved lines were used to map out the eyebrow shape. I used purple, and its complementary colour, yellow, as my two colours. To make the process more convenient, I labelled each layer a particular part of the eye, including eyebrows, so I can edit and navigate easily.

Illustrated Environment

I went with a beautiful nature/sunset image to illustrate. I chose this because these types of photos are my favourite to look at and take of. This type of atmosphere translates through the screen because of how scenic it looks. This was a very ambitious choice for me, and I was worried. Still, I did enjoy trying to capture the essence of the image, mainly using the blob brush tool and different brush definitions while freehanding the entire image, which made the final result feel somewhat abstract. I used 3 colours using the Adobe Colour Wheel, which was a trial and error when it came to illustrating the different blends of colour from the original photo. Labelling each layer was helpful when it came to filling out the backgrounds without disrupting the foreground and middle ground layers.
