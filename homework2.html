Cropping and Resizing Images & Tonal Range

Image A - 1920 x 1080 Crop

Image B - 5 x 7 Crop

I chose Image A because of the horizontally written text as my 1920 x 1080 image. This decision kind of makes the photo look like it scales the image to capture the written text and the direction the text is going in. I liked how the letters were 3D, so when taking the photo, the text looks like it has more depth, and it’s clear but doesn’t necessarily stand out. I used the level tool to brighten the image a bit to make the contents in the image a little clearer, but nothing that drastically changes the image from the original photo. Seeing how the other objects in the image fit in comparison to the text plaque, like the exit sign that’s cut off in the top right corner and the door at the bottom, especially when it came to cropping the image, was interesting. I chose Image B as my 5 x 7 image because of the sizing of the fire hose compartment. I took a photo of this because I was interested in how the depth of the hose would appear in the photo. Since the object is like a locker and there’s a visible section at the top to see inside, I thought it would be cool to see that translate in a 2D image. It was also fun playing around with the level tool and seeing how different highlights reflected on the image, whether that made it a brighter red or a lighter, more ashy red.
