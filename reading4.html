Reading and Writing Response #4

The article “Nuclear War: The Rising Risk, and How We Stop It” finds a way to emphasize its topic through how it’s being read. The JavaScript and CSS styling of the article is uncommon but very impressive and visually pleasing to scroll through. At the beginning of the article, it starts with the text “The risk of nuclear contact is rising,” and the background pulsates red, with this text centered. It creates the feeling of urgency, seriousness, and danger. When you scroll, the question “Is anyone paying attention?” also appears, kind of making the pulsating effect and matter dire. The transitions within the article are very smooth, going from an illustrated style to a typical written article. The article also includes some user interface features, like video clips and having to tap the video for it to unmute or mute. It then goes into another transition effect that brings a narrative to the topic. There are frames of illustrations that make it seem animated in the background of the text that narrates the scenario. The fluidity of the drawings, along with the scrolling of the page, is easy and effortless to look at, but also fascinating. The drawings further the narrative that the editor is trying to stress to the reader. The storytelling is captivating and really keeps your attention throughout the article. It immerses you into it; the animation suddenly feels like you're inside the destructive and difficult scenario people face in real life. The article found a way to visually imitate what people, innocent civilians and bystanders, who have experienced nuclear bombings, go through as it’s happening. It’s like watching a recap or trailer for an animated movie because of how smoothly the illustrated elements blend and move throughout the website. The illustrations and animations obviously downplay the raw reality of what it is showcasing, but it does enough to inform others, giving that representation. As you scroll through the article their are more satisfying visual elements. As you scroll, there may be 4 images in one, and the images will stay in place but highlight the 4 different parts separately, which draws your attention to what it’s trying to show you. The article goes back and forth from a factual, informative text to a more narrative one, and as the tone changes, so does how it looks visually in the article. There’s also a section where, as you scroll, new text pops up along with the background changing as it stays in the same place. The article adds a lot of different graphics to keep the attention and interactive interest of the reader, which is a really captivating but emotional experience, giving the story and purpose of the article. The styling choices definitely elevate the article and, as accurately as it can, depict the intricate events and consequences of a missile fire that not a lot of people were probably aware of. It keeps surprising you with something new, with an important message it’s trying to convey.
